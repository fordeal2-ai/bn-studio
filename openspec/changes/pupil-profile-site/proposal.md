## Why

Create a simple personal website where one pupil can present who they are and share their school work in one place.

## What Changes

- Add a profile section for the pupil's basic information and introduction.
- Add a school subjects section that presents the subjects the pupil studies.
- Add a projects section that showcases the pupil's projects and their details.
- Provide navigation between the three sections and a layout that works on desktop and mobile screens.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `pupil-profile`: Display the pupil's personal profile and introduction.
- `school-subjects`: Display the pupil's school subjects.
- `project-showcase`: Display a collection of the pupil's projects and project details.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

This introduces a new website and its content model. No existing application code, APIs, or integrations are affected.
